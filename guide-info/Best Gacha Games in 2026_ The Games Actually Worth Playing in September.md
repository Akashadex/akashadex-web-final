# 1. Genshin Impact — Best Overall Open-World Gacha

**Best for:** Exploration, story, world-building and casual long-term play

If you want the game that helped define the modern open-world gacha RPG, **Genshin Impact** remains one of the easiest recommendations.

Its biggest strength isn't simply its character collection system.

It's the world.

Teyvat is built around exploration, puzzles, quests, environmental mechanics, hidden locations and large regions that encourage you to wander rather than simply follow a checklist. HoYoverse describes it as an open-world adventure RPG set in Teyvat.

### Why play it?

- Huge explorable world
- Strong soundtrack and presentation
- Large character roster
- Extensive story
- Elemental combat system
- Regular content updates
- Excellent production quality

### The downside

Genshin can be demanding if you're trying to keep up with everything.

The game has years of accumulated content, and players who care about optimizing characters can also get pulled into artifact farming and resource management.

### Verdict

**Still one of the best choices if exploration is your priority.**

**Rating: 9.3/10**

# 2. Wuthering Waves — Best Combat

If Genshin's biggest appeal is exploration, **Wuthering Waves** makes a much stronger argument for combat.

Kuro Games describes it as a story-rich open-world action RPG with a high degree of freedom.

Its combat system emphasizes:

- Dodging
- Parrying
- Character switching
- Combos
- Skill timing
- Enemy mechanics
- Fast movement

The result is a game that feels much more action-oriented than many traditional character-collection RPGs.

And by September 2026, Wuthering Waves has continued receiving substantial updates, including Version 3.6 content scheduled for September.

### Why play it?

- Excellent combat
- Open-world exploration
- Strong character designs
- High mobility
- Generous progression compared with many gachas
- Great option for players who want something more mechanically demanding

### The downside

The game can be more demanding on hardware, and its combat-heavy design won't appeal to people who prefer slower RPGs.

### Verdict

**If combat matters more to you than anything else, start here.**

**Rating: 9.4/10**

# 3. Honkai: Star Rail — Best Turn-Based Gacha

Not every great gacha needs real-time combat.

**Honkai: Star Rail** proves that turn-based RPGs can still be spectacular.

Instead of controlling characters directly during battles, you build a team around:

- Roles
- Weaknesses
- Buffs
- Debuffs
- Speed
- Energy
- Skill points
- Team synergy

This makes Star Rail particularly appealing if you enjoy planning rather than reflex-based combat.

### Why play it?

- Excellent turn-based combat
- Strong characters
- Excellent presentation
- Huge amount of story content
- Easy to play casually
- Good team-building depth

### The downside

Like most long-running live-service games, catching up can feel overwhelming.

It also has a large amount of recurring content, which can turn daily play into a routine if you're not careful.

### Verdict

**The safest recommendation for players who prefer turn-based RPGs.**

**Rating: 9.2/10**

# 5. Arknights: Endfield — Best for Strategy Fans

This is one of the more interesting games in the modern gacha market because it doesn't simply copy the standard open-world formula.

**Arknights: Endfield** combines action RPG combat with strategic systems and industrial/base-building elements.

It launched on January 22, 2026, for PC, mobile and PlayStation 5.

By September, it is already an established 2026 release rather than an upcoming title, and its ongoing updates have continued expanding the game.

### Why play it?

- Interesting strategic mechanics
- Distinctive world
- Industrial/base-building systems
- Action combat
- Strong art direction
- Good choice if conventional open-world gachas are getting repetitive

### The downside

It has more systems to understand than a simple character-collector.

If you want something extremely casual, this may not be your first choice.

### Verdict

**One of the most interesting gachas for players who want something beyond the standard formula.**

**Rating: 9.0/10**

# Which Gacha Games Are Best for F2P Players?

If you don't want to spend money, your priorities should be different.

You shouldn't necessarily choose the game with the prettiest characters.

Look for:

- Generous free currency
- Good pity systems
- Multiple ways to earn pulls
- Strong free characters
- Low power creep
- Useful permanent content
- Limited dependence on duplicates
- Reasonable daily time requirements

Based on those priorities, some of the strongest choices to investigate are:

### 🥇 Duet Night Abyss

Its removal of traditional character and weapon gacha makes it an obvious choice for players who dislike conventional monetization.

### 🥈 Wuthering Waves

Strong combat and a generally generous progression structure make it attractive to F2P players.

### 🥉 Genshin Impact

Its enormous amount of permanent content makes it easy to enjoy without spending, although collecting every character is obviously impossible for most F2P players.

### Honkai: Star Rail

Excellent if you're comfortable focusing on a smaller number of well-built teams rather than collecting everything.

# Which Gacha Should You Avoid?

This is where things become subjective.

A game isn't necessarily bad because its monetization is aggressive.

But you should be cautious if a game combines:

- Heavy power creep
- Multiple layers of RNG
- Expensive duplicates
- Weapon/character banners
- Time-limited progression
- Mandatory daily grinding
- PvP advantages tied to spending

The biggest trap isn't spending money once.

It's building a game routine where **you feel obligated to spend because you've already invested so much time.**

Set a budget before you start.

Not after the banner appears.
