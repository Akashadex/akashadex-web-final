# iPhone 18 Pro, Pro Max & iPhone Duo: Specs, Prices, Features & Release Dates

*Last updated: September 14, 2026*

Apple split its fall lineup for the first time this year: the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and Apple's first foldable, the iPhone Duo, launched in September/October 2026, while the standard iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, and iPhone Air 2 are being held back to spring 2027. Below is a fact-checked breakdown of what was actually announced, what it costs, what changed under the hood, and what's still rumor rather than confirmed.

**Source-status key used throughout:** 🟢 *Apple says* (Apple's own keynote, press release, or spec pages) · 🟡 *Reported* (credible outlet reporting, not yet Apple-confirmed) · 🔴 *Rumored* (leaks/speculation) · ✍️ *Our calculation/analysis* (math or inference we did ourselves, not stated by any source).

## 1. The Event / Basics

🟢 Apple's event, **"Surprise and Shine,"** was held Wednesday, September 9, 2026, at Apple Park. 🟢 It was **John Ternus's first keynote as Apple CEO** — he succeeded Tim Cook on September 1, 2026, with Cook moving into the role of executive chairman.

🟢 Announced: **iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and iPhone Duo** (Apple's first foldable), plus Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and AirPods 5.

🟢 Apple did **not** announce the standard iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, or iPhone Air 2 at this event. 🟡 These are expected in spring 2027.

🟡 Apple simultaneously raised prices on its existing lineup: iPhone 16 → $799 (+$100), iPhone 17e → $699 (+$100), iPhone 17 → $899 (+$100), iPhone Air → $1,099 (+$100).

## 3. Meet the 2026 Models

### iPhone 18 Pro
🟢 6.3-inch display, same footprint as iPhone 17 Pro. Colors: Black, Silver, Glacier, Burgundy. Unibody aluminum frame, Ceramic Shield front, Ceramic Shield 2 rear around the MagSafe cutout — no longer two-tone. Starts at $1,199 (256GB), now goes up to 2TB (previously Pro Max-exclusive).

### iPhone 18 Pro Max
🟢 6.9-inch display, same design/colors as the Pro. Starts at $1,299 (256GB). 🟢 Apple's longest iPhone battery life to date: up to 45 hours video playback, 30 hours typical use. ✍️ Slightly heavier than the 17 Pro Max (249g vs an unchanged footprint) due to a bigger battery and new cooling system, not a size increase.

### iPhone Duo (foldable — Apple's first)
🟢 Book-style fold; 7.6-inch inner foldable OLED + 5.4-inch outer OLED, both up to 120Hz. Grade 5 titanium hinge, Ceramic Shield front/back, IP68 — a first for a book-style foldable. Same A20 Pro chip as the Pro line. 🟢 Touch ID in the side button instead of Face ID (it still has an under-display FaceTime camera). No telephoto, no LiDAR, no variable aperture. Colors: Star White, Night Sky only. Starts at $1,999 (256GB), up to $3,199 (2TB).

## 5. What Actually Changed — Deep Dive

### Performance (A20 Pro chip) 🟢
- Apple's first chip on TSMC's 2nm process — a full node jump from the A19 Pro's 3nm.
- 6-core CPU (2 new "super cores" + 4 efficiency cores) — up to 20% faster.
- New 7-core GPU with per-core Neural Accelerators — up to 40% faster graphics, 2x FP8 throughput.
- Dual 16-core Neural Engine (32 cores total) — Apple says this doubles on-device AI throughput vs. the single 16-core A19 Pro engine.
- 50% more memory bandwidth than the A19 Pro.
- New packaging (WMCM) puts memory beside, not on top of, the die, letting the silicon contact the cooling system directly.
- Redesigned vapor chamber has roughly 3x the surface area of the 17 Pro's — Apple says this yields up to 40% better sustained performance under load.
- ✍️ Real-world read: everyday tasks won't feel different; the gains show up in gaming, video editing, and heavy on-device AI.

### Cameras 🟢
- First iPhone with a variable aperture, on the 48MP Fusion Main camera only: four stops — f/1.48, f/1.8 (default), f/2.8, f/4.0 — driven by six laser-cut blades on a single rotor.
- New Pro manual controls: aperture, shutter speed, white balance, adjustable in-app.
- 48MP Ultra Wide (macro-capable) + Fusion Telephoto (Apple's multi-focal-length zoom from a single sensor, not a separate optical zoom lens).
- New "Apple Reference Image" feature signs each pixel to flag AI edits.
- 4K 120fps Dolby Vision video; Cinematic effects now applicable after capture, even on 60fps clips; Audio Mix adds music isolation.
- Pro video tools continue: ProRes RAW, Apple Log 2, Genlock, Dual Capture.

### Battery 🟢
- 18 Pro: up to 24h typical / 36h video / 33h streamed.
- 18 Pro Max: up to 30h typical / 45h video / 40h streamed — Apple's best-ever iPhone battery life.
- Duo: up to 44h video on the outer display, 31h on the inner, 24h when both are used equally.
- Charging: 50% in ~15 min via 60W-capable adapter (wired); 50% in ~30 min with a 35W-or-higher adapter over MagSafe/Qi2 (corrected from an earlier 25W figure — Apple's spec page lists 35W). Five minutes on a cable ≈ 7 hours of video on the Pro Max.

### Display 🟢
- Same panel sizes as the 17 Pro line — no size change. Smaller Dynamic Island, now shows 3 Live Activities at once.
- 3,000 nits peak outdoor brightness, 1-nit minimum for Always-On.
- 🟡 Panels reportedly supplied by both Samsung Display and LG Display using Samsung's newer **M16** OLED material set for better efficiency, longevity, and color accuracy — confirmed in use on both the Pro line and the Duo's displays, per Samsung Display's own M16 announcement.
- 🟡 No under-display Face ID this year — reportedly being saved for a redesigned "iPhone 20" anniversary model expected fall 2027.

### AI / Software 🟢
- Ships with iOS 27 (released Sept 14, 2026).
- Rebuilt **Siri AI**, developed via Apple's collaboration with Google — 🟡 reports describe backend ties to Google Gemini. Runs as a standalone app; can use personal context across Messages/Mail/Photos, act on-screen, work inside Camera, and write on your behalf.
- Rolling out in beta Sept 14 for English-set devices; French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish arriving October.
- 🟡 The deepest Siri AI tier is reportedly gated to A17 Pro or newer chips.
- Satellite connectivity for off-grid situations.

### Design 🟢
Same general language as the 17 Pro — unibody aluminum, camera plateau, Action button, Camera Control, USB-C. Visible changes: smaller Dynamic Island, new Burgundy color, blended (non-two-tone) back, and a **slightly easier EU repairability rating than the 17 Pro** (both land in class C on the EU's A–E scale, but Apple's filing shows small gains in tool access and disassembly depth).

### Other 🟢/🟡
- New Apple-designed **C2 modem** (faster uploads, ~15% less power than C1X, adds mmWave 5G in the US). 🟢 Confirmed: the iPhone 18 Pro uses C2 in every market, including the US. The iPhone 18 Pro Max uses C2 everywhere **except** the US, where it uses a Qualcomm modem instead (likely the Snapdragon X80, unconfirmed pending teardown). The Duo uses C2 worldwide.
- New N1 chip: Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread, 2nd-gen Ultra Wideband.

## 7. Is the iPhone Duo Worth It?

At $1,999 it undercuts some pre-launch rumors ($2,000–$3,000+) but is still Apple's most expensive iPhone. **What Pro Max buyers don't get:** a 7.6" foldable canvas, new multitasking built for the form factor, IP68 (a first for a book-style foldable), titanium hinge build. **What you give up versus the 18 Pro Max:** Touch ID instead of Face ID, no telephoto, no LiDAR, no variable aperture. ✍️ Bottom line: you're paying for form factor and multitasking, not for a camera or biometric upgrade over the Pro Max.

## 8. Should You Upgrade?

*(consensus across Digital Trends, MacRumors, WhistleOut, Technary, Smartish)*

**From iPhone 17 Pro:** Broad "no general-purpose reason to upgrade" — design, size, and everyday camera use are largely unchanged, for $100 more. Worth it if you specifically want the variable-aperture camera + manual controls, or push sustained performance (gaming, video/photo editing, heavy on-device AI). 🟡 One MacRumors commenter noted that strong iPhone 17 Pro trade-in values can shrink the net cost of upgrading — a value point, not a features point.

**From iPhone 16 Pro or older:** Easier to recommend — newer chip, camera system, and a longer runway before the next model, since the standard iPhone 18 isn't landing until spring 2027.

**✍️ Our extrapolation, not stated by any source we found:** for an iPhone 15 Pro or an iPhone 14 or older, the jump is likely even more noticeable across camera, performance, and battery — but we haven't seen this stated outright anywhere, so treat it as reasoned inference rather than reported consensus.

**General verdict:** upgrade if you're 2+ generations behind, or you use the specific upgraded features professionally/heavily. Skip if you're on a 17 Pro doing typical daily tasks.

# FAQ

*Answers below add detail not already covered above rather than repeating it — see the relevant section for full context.*

**Is the iPhone Duo Apple's first foldable iPhone?**
🟢 Yes — Apple's first-ever foldable, after years of speculation about names like "iPhone Ultra" or "iPhone Fold."

**What's the difference between iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max?**
🟢 Mainly size and battery (6.3" vs 6.9"; up to 36h vs 45h video). Chip and camera system are identical.

**What does the iPhone Duo actually lack compared with the Pro Max?**
🟢 Face ID (it uses Touch ID in the side button instead, but still has an under-display front camera), the telephoto lens, LiDAR, and variable aperture. ✍️ These are the meaningful trade-offs; we're intentionally not reducing this to a headline number, since a "10 features" framing doesn't reflect which differences actually matter day to day.

**Does the iPhone 18 Pro's battery capacity vary by country?**
🟢 Yes — the published hour figures apply only to the eSIM-only models (US, Canada, Mexico, Japan, and a few others); markets with a physical SIM tray get a smaller battery, and Apple hasn't published separate figures for those.

**Does every iPhone 18 Pro Max use the same modem?**
🟢 No — every Pro Max uses Apple's C2 modem except the US version, which uses a Qualcomm modem instead. The smaller iPhone 18 Pro uses C2 everywhere, including the US.

**Is the iPhone 18 Pro actually easier to repair than the 17 Pro?**
🟢 Slightly — Apple's own EU filing shows small gains in tool access and disassembly depth, though both land in the same repairability class (C) on the EU's A–E scale.

**Why is the iPhone 18 Pro Max heavier this year if it's not thicker?**
🟢 A larger battery and a roughly 3x larger vapor chamber, not a size change — the footprint matches the 17 Pro Max exactly.

**Is trading in an iPhone 17 Pro Max for a Duo or 18 Pro a good deal?**
🟢 Apple's current published trade-in range tops out at $885 for an iPhone 17 Pro Max in good condition — the highest figure in its range, but well short of covering the $1,999 Duo or even the $1,299 Pro Max outright. Apple doesn't publish per-model values as a fixed table; the $885 figure is the recorded ceiling from its trade-in questionnaire, not a guaranteed quote.

**Is the 2TB storage option new to the iPhone 18 Pro, or was it already available?**
🟢 New — last year 2TB was Pro Max-exclusive; this year the standard Pro offers it too.

**Could Apple discontinue the standard iPhone 18 entirely?**
🔴 There's a single low-confidence rumor (one X account, uncorroborated) suggesting this. Multiple outlets and a separate supply-chain report contradict it. Treat it as debunked-leaning, not a real possibility.

**Should I wait for the 2027 iPhone 18?**
✍️ Only if you specifically want the standard (non-Pro) model or a lower price point. If you want the best camera and performance available now, the 18 Pro is already shipping.
